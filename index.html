About the Fair

Welcome to Commonwealth Academy's STEM Fair, hosted by Brentan Rath. This event is designed to empower your children and ignite their passion for science, technology, engineering and mathematics.

Over three exciting days, you'll have the opportunity to explore hundreds of interactive exhibits, attend engaging workshops, and witness groundbreaking demonstrations. From robotics and artificial intelligence to sustainable energy and biotechnology, our fair covers the full spectrum of STEM disciplines.

Whether you're a student passionate about science, a parent supporting your child's education, or a professional looking to connect with the next generation of innovators, STEM Fair 2026 offers something for everyone. Join us in inspiring curiosity, fostering creativity, and shaping the future of technology.
