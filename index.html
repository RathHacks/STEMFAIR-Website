About the Fair

Ignite your child’s curiosity and passion, as well as prepare them for the world of tomorrow! The Commonwealth Academy STEM Fair is more than just an event; it is an immersive experience designed to empower the next generation of innovators, thinkers, and problem-solvers, just like we know your kids are.

Why this event?

Over the course of four hours, your child will be introduced to the foundational pillars of Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM). We have curated a high-energy, safe, and engaging environment where complex concepts become hands-on adventures through workshops. Whether your child is a budding scientist or, junior computer genius or just discovering these fields for the first time, our goal is to capture their imagination and keep them inspired long after the event ends with the many resources we will provide you.

Future-Ready Skills

If you are passionate about preparing your child for a rapidly evolving world, this is the place to be. On top of empowering their journey to future proof high paying jobs, we bridge the gap in-between the classroom learning environment and a hands-on STEM environment. All building up to prepare you kid to understand the evolving technologies of today, that will be the future of tomorrow. Such as:

  • Artificial Intelligence (AI)
  • Robotics
  • Biotechnology
  • Quantum Computing

What to Expect:

  • Hands-On Workshops: Interactive stations where kids can build, learn, and experiment.
  • Expert Mentorship: Guidance from passionate facilitators who make learning relatable.
  • Collaborative Challenges: Team-based activities that build critical thinking and communication skills.
